# Competitor design research

Navigation labels, page structure and action placement were reviewed from indexed public pages on 19 November 2026. The findings below concern information architecture and content, rather than a visual audit of each site in a browser.

## Patterns applied to MSL

| Site | Observed pattern | MSL design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Newcross Healthcare](https://www.newcrosshealthcare.com/) | Distinct care-professional and care-organisation navigation beside a direct job action. | Give candidates and clients separate menus, homepage entry points and dedicated enquiry journeys. |
| [Medacs Healthcare](https://www.medacs.com/) | Job discovery, CV submission and client services appear near the introduction. | Put work and staffing actions above the fold. Present registration as its own task. |
| [JustNurses](https://www.justnurses.co.uk/) | Apply, telephone and email sit alongside nursing categories by geography and specialty. | Put a direct way to contact a consultant beside the registration and discipline pages. |
| [Globe Locums](https://www.globelocums.co.uk/) | Candidate registration, compliance guidance and jobs have distinct destinations. | Let a visitor read the document checklist before contacting the recruitment team. |
| [Pulse Jobs](https://www.pulsejobs.com/) | Sector navigation and candidate information organise a broad service range. | Group MSL’s actual disciplines on a service page and cross-link jobs and compliance. |

## Additional references

| Site | Observed feature and relevance |
| --- | --- |
| [QAR Group](https://qargroup.ie/) | The homepage fetch returned a cache miss. An indexed careers result labels roles by location and work pattern. Visual treatment could not be verified. [Careers](https://qargroup.ie/careers/) |
| [HSA Locums](https://hsalocums.com/) | Separates jobs, candidates and clients. Accreditations have a dedicated navigation entry. |
| [Military Medical Personnel](https://www.militarymedicalpersonnel.com/) | Places registration within candidate navigation and describes the precise service audience in the introduction. |
| [Ambition24hours International](https://www.ambition24hours.com/) | Separates client facilities and recruitment under Staffing and Jobs. Apply is directly accessible. |
| [Ambition24hours UK](https://ambition24hours.co.uk/) | Pairs registration with practical nursing resources and gives employers their own staffing navigation. |
| [Ambition24direct](https://www.a24direct.co.uk/) | Groups specialist care topics by service. This organisation’s patient-care catalogue differs from MSL’s staffing remit. |
| [ID Medical](https://www.id-medical.com/) | Profession-specific navigation connects visitors to both disciplines and support. |
| [Day Webster](https://www.daywebster.com/) | Puts specialty discovery alongside callback and registration actions. |
| [Your World](https://www.yourworld.com/uk) | Makes location and role separate discovery inputs. Those details also help consultants answer an enquiry. |
| [Mayday Healthcare](https://www.maydayhealthcareplc.co.uk/) | Offers nursing job search, telephone and callback routes for visitors with different needs. |

MSL’s published vacancies page uses consultant enquiries, so vacancy listings and filters require a verified feed. Only MSL’s documented services, claims and credentials belong in its public-facing content.
